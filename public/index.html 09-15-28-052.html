Pathmark partners with educational researchers and religious organizations to deliver survey-based research and grant programs directly through WhatsApp — meeting communities in the tools they already use.

Educational Research Partnerships We collaborate with universities and independent researchers to design, distribute, and analyze community surveys.
Religious & Community Organizations We work alongside faith communities to support grant programs and gather outcomes data from the populations they serve.
Grants & Impact Measurement CG Charitable funds charitable initiatives and uses Pathmark to measure outcomes and report on program effectiveness.
